Welcome to LEXIMOVE: Moving Words into Meaning through Actions and Deep Exploration!

This book is designed to help young learners embark on an exciting journey of learning English through engaging and meaningful activities rooted in the Total Physical Response (TPR) method. By combining language learning with physical movement, TPR helps children understand and remember vocabulary and commands more naturally, making the process both enjoyable and effective.

Each unit in this book presents practical themes relevant to children's daily lives, such as greetings, classroom actions, routines, and common objects. Activities are carefully structured to support listening, speaking, reading, and simple writing skills—while also encouraging active participation and joyful learning.

The materials in this book align with the English curriculum for elementary schools in Indonesia and aim to foster communicative competence from an early age. With colorful illustrations, fun exercises, and clear instructions, students will not only learn English but also build confidence in using it actively.

We hope this book serves as a valuable resource for teachers, students, and parents alike in creating a dynamic and supportive learning environment.
