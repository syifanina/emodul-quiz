"Let's Tidy Up The Classroom!"

Help me to tidy up our classroom.
Put the book on the table.
Put the shoes under the table.
Put the pencil case in the bag.
Place the pencil between the red book and the blue book.
Hang the picture above the white board.
Hang the jacket behind the door.
Now, sit beside me.
We sit near the window.
