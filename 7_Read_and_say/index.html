LET'S PREPARE YOUR FAVORITE FRUITS AND VEGETABLES

Today Rani helps her mother cooking in the kitchen.
She says, “Let’s prepare your favorite fruits and vegetables!
First, we will make a fruit salad.
Wash the apples
Peel the bananas
Peel the oranges
Cut the watermelon
Divide the dragonfruit
Slice the melon
Peel the mangoes
Peel the pineapple
Scoop the flesh of the avocados
And cut the papayas
Now help me make a vegetable soup!
Cut the broccoli
Wash the spinach
Shred the carrot
Peel the onions
Chop the garlic
Cut the scallion
Dice the potatoes
Wash the tomatoes
Peel the corns
And wash the lettuce"
Great job, Rani.
We have made a fruit salad and vegetable soup.
Now you can eat your favorite fruits and vegetables!
