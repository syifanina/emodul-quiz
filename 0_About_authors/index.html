Dr. Nidya Chandra Muji Utami, S.Pd., M.Si.

Head Lecturer in English Education
Universitas Negeri Jakarta

Dr. Nidya Chandra Muji Utami, S.Pd., M.Si. is a head lecturer at the Elementary Teacher Education Program, Faculty of Education, Universitas Negeri Jakarta. Her expertise focuses on English language learning for elementary school students.

Her academic work explores vocabulary learning, Total Physical Response (TPR), digital learning media, and the development of English learning materials for elementary education. Through her academic contributions, she supports the development of meaningful, engaging, and child-friendly English learning experiences for young learners.

Prof. Dr. Ika Lestari, S.Pd., M.Si.

Professor of Educational Technology
Universitas Negeri Jakarta

Prof. Dr. Ika Lestari, S.Pd., M.Si. is a professor at the Elementary Teacher Education Program, Faculty of Education, Universitas Negeri Jakarta. Her expertise focuses on educational technology, communication, and the design of educational media.

Her academic work explores technology-supported learning, digital learning media, and innovative learning design for elementary education. Through her academic contributions, she supports the development of meaningful, engaging, and accessible learning experiences for young learners.
